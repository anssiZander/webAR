Bridging the Physical and Digital Universe.

An interactive blueprint of the AR Science Gallery companion app. By leveraging WebAR, spatial tracking, and gamification, we transform static museum exhibits into dynamic, multi-dimensional learning environments without the friction of traditional app downloads.

The Frictionless Launch

The journey begins at the museum foyer. The critical hurdle for any digital museum companion is the initial adoption phase. Traditional mobile apps require finding the app store, downloading heavy files on potentially slow cellular networks, and creating accounts.

By utilizing WebAR, the visitor simply scans a QR code using their default camera app. A single tap launches the experience instantly. The chart compares the projected retention funnel of our Frictionless WebAR approach versus a traditional App Store download model.

The Spatial Narrative Flow

How visitors navigate the hybrid environment, from initial wayfinding to deep-dive interactions.

1

QR Scan & WebAR Load

User points their native camera at the welcome kiosk. A WebAR link pops up, bridging the physical and digital worlds instantly with a spinning atom loading screen.

👣

Spatial Wayfinding

Glowing AR footprints appear superimposed on the physical floor. A mini-map and navigation bar guide the user 50ft ahead to the Astrophysics Wing.

🔍

Marker Recognition

The user arrives at the physical Sun exhibit. The app's reticle locks onto a geometric AR marker below the plaque, prompting the user to step back.

🌌

The Magic Reveal (Spawn)

3D holographic planets spawn, orbiting the physical Sun pedestal in real-time. The digital content remains perfectly locked in spatial perspective as the user walks around.

📲

Deep Dive Interaction

Tapping a glowing hotspot near Jupiter pauses the orbit. A semi-transparent info panel reveals a 3D cross-section of the core, audio playback, and comparison toggles.

Exhibit Engagement Profile

Once the AR experience spawns around a physical object, user behavior shifts. The application is designed not just for flashy visuals, but for layered educational engagement.

This visualization breaks down the projected distribution of time spent during an active exhibit interaction. A significant portion of time is dedicated to exploring the spatial relationships of the 3D models (Orbit Observation), followed closely by reading the multi-media Info Panels triggered by interactive hotspots.

  • ■ Spatial Immersion: Walking around the physical pedestal.
  • ■ Deep Learning: Reading "Did you know?" popups.
  • ■ Navigation: Following AR footprints to the next node.
